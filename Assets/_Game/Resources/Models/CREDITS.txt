Third-party art used in Real Golf
=================================

All models in this folder are by Kenney (www.kenney.nl) and are released under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0 Universal), meaning they are free for personal,
educational and commercial use with no attribution required.

Crediting Kenney is optional but encouraged, so: thanks, Kenney.

  Nature Kit  -  https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit
    Trees/tree_*.fbx
    Props/rock_*.fbx, grass*.fbx, flower_*.fbx

  Minigolf Kit  -  https://kenney.nl/assets/minigolf-kit
    Golf/ball-*.fbx, club-*.fbx, flag-*.fbx, hole-round.fbx
    Golf/Textures/colormap.png

License: http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Everything else in the project (terrain meshes, materials, course layouts) is
generated procedurally at runtime by the RealGolf scripts.
