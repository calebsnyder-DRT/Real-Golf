Modifications made for this project
-----------------------------------
The source scans are not usable as shipped (island_tree_01 alone is 1,599,403
triangles and sits on a scanned rock). Every asset here has been reprocessed:

1. Ground bases removed. island_tree_01 and jacaranda_tree were scanned sitting
   on a rock/dirt slab that shares a material with the trunk, so the slab was cut
   away geometrically rather than by material. Every asset now has a flat bottom
   sitting exactly on z=0 with its origin at the centre of the trunk base.

2. LOD chains. Each FBX contains three meshes, <asset>_LOD0/1/2. Woody geometry
   is collapse-decimated; leaf and needle geometry is not, because collapsing
   alpha cards destroys them. Instead each card's redundant interior tessellation
   is dissolved (the outline and UVs survive), whole cards are then dropped at
   random, and the survivors are scaled about their own centroids to keep the
   canopy opaque.

3. Alpha. Poly Haven ships the leaf cutout as a separate *_alpha map. It has been
   composited into the alpha channel of the matching *_diff.png, and colour has
   been bled outward under the cut texels so mipmaps do not darken leaf edges.

4. Colour grading. The source scans are Mediterranean yellow-olive. Leaf, shrub
   and turf diffuse maps were regraded in linear light toward the target art
   direction (a saturated, distinctly yellow-green mid-green), and bark was warmed
   from cold grey toward brown.

5. Textures downsampled from 2K and repacked (diffuse 512-1024, normals 512,
   roughness 256-512) to keep the whole set small.

Texture suffixes are _diff / _nor / _rough. They deliberately avoid _albedo and
_normal, which BakedTexturePostprocessor already claims across
Resources/Models/. Import settings for this folder are owned by
Assets/_Game/Scripts/Editor/NatureTexturePostprocessor.cs.


Real-Golf - stylized vegetation (Nature/Stylized)
=================================================

Source
------
Stylized Nature MegaKit (Standard/free tier), by Quaternius
(https://quaternius.com/packs/stylizednaturemegakit.html), released under
CC0 1.0 Universal. No attribution is legally required; it is given here as a
courtesy. 68 of the pack's 116 models ship in the free tier, and all 68 are
installed here.

    Folder      Models  Contents
    ----------- ------- ----------------------------------------------
    Trees            20 CommonTree, Pine, DeadTree, TwistedTree (5 each)
    Shrubs           11 bushes, ferns, plants, clovers, mushrooms
    Grass             4 common and wispy, short and tall
    Flowers           9 flower groups/singles and loose petals
    Rocks            24 boulders, pebbles, stepping-stone paths

These are a different art direction from the photoscanned set above: flat-lit
stylized geometry with hand-painted atlases, not regraded scans. They are kept
in Stylized/ so the two can be chosen per hole rather than mixed by accident.

Modifications made for this project
-----------------------------------
1. The pack's "FBX (Unity)" variants are used, not the plain FBX set, so the
   axis conversion is already correct for Unity.

2. Textures renamed onto this folder's _diff / _nor convention, keyed on the
   FBX material names so NatureTexturePostprocessor resolves them with no new
   code. The pack ships each leaf sheet twice: a white alpha-only mask and a
   coloured "_C" variant. The "_C" variants are the real albedo and are the
   ones installed as _diff:

       Bark_NormalTree.png          -> Bark_NormalTree_diff.png
       Bark_NormalTree_Normal.png   -> Bark_NormalTree_nor.png
       Bark_DeadTree.png            -> Bark_DeadTree_diff.png
       Bark_DeadTree_Normal.png     -> Bark_DeadTree_nor.png
       Bark_TwistedTree.png         -> Bark_TwistedTree_diff.png
       Bark_TwistedTree_Normal.png  -> Bark_TwistedTree_nor.png
       Leaves_NormalTree_C.png      -> Leaves_NormalTree_diff.png
       Leaf_Pine_C.png              -> Leaves_Pine_diff.png
       Leaves_TwistedTree_C.png     -> Leaves_TwistedTree_diff.png
       Leaves.png                   -> Leaves_diff.png
       Flowers.png                  -> Flowers_diff.png
       Grass.png                    -> Grass_diff.png
       Mushrooms.png                -> Mushrooms_diff.png
       Rocks_Diffuse.png            -> Rocks_diff.png
       PathRocks_Diffuse.png        -> PathRocks_diff.png

   Only the bark maps ship normals; the foliage and rock sheets are diffuse
   only, so those materials import flat-normalled. No _rough maps exist, which
   is what NatureTexturePostprocessor already assumes.

3. No LOD chains. Unlike the photoscanned assets these are 1.6k-10k triangles
   as shipped, so they import as a single mesh and OnPostprocessModel's
   _LOD0/1/2 wiring is skipped. TwistedTree is the heavy one (~10k tris) and
   wants an LOD pass before it is scattered in quantity.

Known gaps
----------
Bush_Common.fbx carries its woody stems on a material literally named
"Material", so there is no Material_diff.png for it to resolve and the stems
import untextured white. The leaf half of that model is fine. Either rename the
material in the FBX or drop a Material_diff.png alongside the others.

TwistedTree's leaf atlas is autumn red, not green. That is the shipped colour,
not an import fault; point Leaves_TwistedTree_diff.png at a green sheet if a
summer variant is wanted.
