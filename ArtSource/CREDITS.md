## Pro Shop (`Assets/_Game/Art/ProShop/Models/`, `Blender/PuttshackPro_ProShop.blend`)

All Pro Shop geometry is procedurally modelled by `Blender/scripts/pp_proshop*.py`: the room, the course outside the windows, the props, the folded stacks, socks, belt, clubs and all three golf bags. No new third-party assets were downloaded.

| Export | Derived from | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `Display_Polo_Hanger`, `Display_Tee_Hanger`, `Display_Shoes_Pair` | Caleb modular parts (`Top_Polo`, `Top_Tee`, `Shoes_Golf`), which build on Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men (see above). Posed, flattened and re-meshed for display. | CC0 1.0 |
| `Display_MannequinHead` | Lathe profile sized from the Caleb head proportions | CC0 1.0 |

The Pro Shop review renders under `Review/ProShop/blender/` use the Poly Haven Kloofendal HDRI listed above as the sky (CC0 1.0).

## Pebble Beach course (`Assets/_Game/Art/Course/`, `Assets/_Game/Resources/Models/Course/`, `Blender/PuttshackPro_PebbleBeach.blend`)

All geometry is procedurally modelled by `Blender/scripts/pp_pebble*.py`: the surround terrain, cliffs, beach and sea floor of Stillwater Cove, rocks and sea stacks, the Lodge and houses, the pier and boats, the Monterey cypress (hero models and scenery), the distant forest, the ocean plane and the tee markers. The ocean wave/foam maps and the cypress leaf-clump maps (`foliage_normal.png`, `foliage_noise.png`) are generated by the same script. Source downloads live in `Blender/textures/pebble/`.

| Asset | Used for | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poly Haven — Kloppenheim 06 (Pure Sky), 4k HDR | `pebble_sky.hdr`: course skybox, ambient and reflections | https://polyhaven.com/a/kloppenheim_06_puresky | CC0 1.0 |
| Poly Haven — Cliff Side, 2k diffuse + GL normal | `rock_albedo.png` / `rock_normal.png`: triplanar bluff and cliff layer of the ground shader | https://polyhaven.com/a/cliff_side | CC0 1.0 |
| Poly Haven — Coast Sand 01, 2k diffuse + GL normal | `beach_albedo.png` / `beach_normal.png`: beach and wet-sand layer | https://polyhaven.com/a/coast_sand_01 | CC0 1.0 |
